COMPANY OVERVIEW

Who We Are, Our Heritage & National Footprint

Makhaswa Holdings (Pty) Ltd is a 100% Black-Owned, Level 1 B-BBEE accredited civil engineering, building, and infrastructure contractor headquartered in Pretoria, Gauteng, with active operational capacity across Gauteng, Limpopo, KwaZulu-Natal, and North West.

Company Heritage & Foundation

Officially incorporated on 17 July 2017, the company has evolved from residential and municipal subcontracts into a highly capable Grade 8 multi-disciplinary contractor trusted by public sector authorities, metros, and private commercial developers.

Engineering Scope & Capability

Specialised in bulk earthworks, municipal road layerworks, continuous-graded asphalt surfacing, deep stormwater systems, bulk potable water reticulation, and turnkey structural building facilities.

CORE DISCIPLINES

Engineering Capabilities & Technical Scope

01. Bulk Earthworks & Site Prep

Clearing, 300mm topsoil stripping, cut-to-fill balancing, mass terrace preparation, basement excavation, subgrade ripping to 90%–93% Mod AASHTO, and lime/cement stabilization.

02. Roads & Layerworks Surfacing

G7/G5 subgrade, stabilized C3/C4 sub-base, and G1 crushed base compacted to 98%–102% Mod AASHTO. Continuous-graded asphalt, slurry seals, and 60mm/80mm interlocking paving.

03. Stormwater & Deep Drainage

Supply and laying of 450mm–1200mm concrete pipes, Class 100D Ogee pipes, portal box culverts, in-situ concrete manholes, JRA kerb inlets, and subsoil drainage networks.

04. Potable Water Reticulation

uPVC, mPVC, and HDPE bulk potable water distribution pipelines, manifold valve chambers, hydrostatic pressure testing, disinfection, and household erf connections.

05. General Building & Housing

NHBRC Class 5 registered builder. Turnkey delivery of educational facilities, clinics, municipal depot offices, ablutions, and multi-storey residential developments.

06. Electrical & High-Mast Lighting

Reinforced concrete plinths, high-mast lighting pole erection, luminaire mounting, LV/MV cabling, and high-security perimeter fencing (palisade and weldmesh).

SHEQ FRAMEWORK

Safety, Quality Testing & Environmental Governance
Health & Safety (OHS)

Zero-Harm Policy: Strict adherence to OHS Act 85/1993 and Construction Regs 2014. Daily toolbox talks, mandatory SABS PPE compliance, Pr.OHS safety officers, and full incident management registers.

Quality Testing (QMS)

ISO 9001 Alignment: Inspection & Test Plans (ITPs). Troxler compaction checks, concrete slump tests, and 7-, 14-, and 28-day compression cube crushing at SANAS-accredited testing laboratories.

Environmental (EMS)

ISO 14001 Alignment: Silt trapping, stormwater runoff control berms, bunded hazardous fuel storage, waste manifests, topsoil stockpiling, and full post-construction site rehabilitation.

SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITY

Socio-Economic Development & B-BBEE Transformation

At Makhaswa Holdings, socio-economic transformation is embedded into every civil contract. We measure success by the tangible technical capacity left behind in host communities:

Local Economic Development (LED)

Prioritised recruitment of 30% to 50% local labour from project host communities, combined with structured on-the-job training in layerworks, pipe laying, concrete shuttering, and machine operations.

Enterprise & SMME Development

Ring-fencing specialized trade packages (paving, kerbing, painting, fencing, security) for local emerging SMMEs, paired with practical mentorship in pricing, health and safety, and quality delivery.

Youth & Women Artisan Empowerment

Targeted inclusion of female and youth artisans across technical and supervisory site roles, providing certified learnerships, machine operator certifications, and career advancement paths.

Corporate Social Investment (CSI)

Direct sponsorship of local school facilities (e.g. Diepsloot West Secondary School), community sports tournaments, and donation of building materials for vulnerable community centers.
