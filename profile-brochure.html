Who We Are

Makhaswa Holdings (Pty) Ltd is a fully black-owned civil engineering and construction company headquartered in Gauteng, South Africa, with operational offices in Midrand, Pretoria, Mokopane, and Durban.

Officially registered on 17 July 2017, the company has grown into a reputable multi-disciplinary contractor trusted by both public and private sector clients for the successful delivery of infrastructure and construction projects.

With a strong focus on quality, efficiency, and reliability, Makhaswa Holdings consistently delivers projects on time and within budget while contributing to sustainable community development.

"Live Beyond Measures — Delivering precision engineering, compliant infrastructure, and lasting value for our clients and host communities."

Core Services

Earthworks & Site Clearance

Mass earthworks, site leveling with heavy machinery, and large-scale site preparation for robust foundations.

Civil Works & Drainage

Implementation of storm-water drainage, sewer pipelines, concrete box culverts, and structural concrete installations.

Road Construction & Surfacing

Kerbing, asphalt surfacing, rehabilitation of existing roads, paved walkways, and full road network construction.

General Building

Construction of residential and commercial buildings, low-income housing developments, and high-quality solid brickwork structures.

Electrical & Mechanical

Installations, power systems, HVAC setups, and mechanical building systems tailored for efficiency and reliability.

Project Management

Comprehensive project lifecycle management from conception and planning to execution and final handover.
