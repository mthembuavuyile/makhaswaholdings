# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & CORPORATE IDENTITY

### 1.1 Who We Are
Makhaswa Holdings (Pty) Ltd is a premier, 100% Black-Owned, Level 1 B-BBEE multi-disciplinary civil engineering, infrastructure development, mechanical, electrical, and building construction enterprise headquartered in Gauteng, South Africa. The company was officially incorporated with the Companies and Intellectual Property Commission (CIPC) on **Monday, 17 July 2017**.

Over years of sustained operational growth and strategic capital investment, Makhaswa Holdings has scaled into a high-tier contractor holding elite **CIDB Grade 8 CE PE** (Civil Engineering) and **Grade 8 ME PE** (Mechanical Engineering) accreditations, alongside active gradings in General Building (6GB PE), Specialist Building Works (7SB PE), and Electrical Infrastructure (7EB PE). The firm delivers public sector infrastructure tenders, municipal framework contracts, commercial developments, healthcare and educational facilities, and bulk municipal reticulation schemes throughout South Africa.

### 1.2 Our Vision
To be a leading African infrastructure and engineering contractor recognized for execution excellence, innovative engineering solutions, uncompromised safety standards, and sustainable socio-economic development.

### 1.3 Our Mission
To procure and execute construction projects at competitive pricing, provide safe and compliant working environments, deliver quality infrastructure on time and within budget, and empower local communities through meaningful skills transfer, employment creation, and economic inclusion.

### 1.4 Core Corporate Values
* **Integrity:** Operating with absolute transparency, honesty, fairness, and ethical corporate governance across all client, supplier, and regulatory dealings.
* **Excellence:** Dedicated to technical precision, top-tier workmanship, strict specification compliance, and punctual project handover.
* **Safety First (Zero Harm):** Uncompromising commitment to the health, safety, and physical well-being of our workforce, subcontractors, clients, and surrounding communities.
* **Empowerment & Transformation:** Driving broad-based black economic empowerment through local recruitment, artisan development, and mentorship of emerging SMMEs.
* **Sustainability:** Applying construction methodologies that are environmentally sound, energy-efficient, resource-conscious, and socially uplifting.
* **Professionalism:** Maintaining structured project planning, transparent stakeholder communication, clear schedules, and rigorous quality assurance.

### 1.5 Corporate Philosophy
> *"We don't just construct buildings — we create lasting legacies. From concept to completion, we bring our clients' visions to life with precision, passion, and excellence under our guiding principle: Live Beyond Measures."*

### 6.1 Earthworks & Bulk Ground Engineering
* **Site Clearance & Demolition:** Clearing and grubbing, topsoil stripping and stockpiling, structural demolition, tree felling, and rubble removal.
* **Bulk Excavation & Mass Earthworks:** Cut-to-fill balancing, mass terrace preparation, basement excavation, deep trenching in soft, intermediate, and hard rock conditions.
* **Subgrade Preparation:** Roadbed preparation, ripping and re-compaction to 90%–93% Mod AASHTO, lime/cement stabilization, and dump rock filling for sub-surface stabilization in wet/marshy ground.

### 6.2 Civil Works & Municipal Stormwater Infrastructure
* **Stormwater Reticulation:** Supply, trench excavation, flexible class bedding, jointing, laying, and testing of spigot and socket concrete pipes (diameters: 450mm, 525mm, 600mm, 675mm, 750mm, 825mm, 900mm, 1050mm, and 1200mm).
* **Ogee Concrete Culverts:** Installation of Class 100D Ogee pipes and precast reinforced concrete portal/box culverts for stormwater stream crossings and bridge bypasses.
* **Drainage Structures:** Construction of in-situ reinforced concrete and brickwork manholes (complete with Type 4A / Type 2A heavy-duty ductile iron / polymer covers and frames), stepped manholes with drop chambers, catchpits, and standard JRA kerb inlets (drawings JRA-SD-SW-020 to 024).
* **Subsoil Drainage Systems:** 110mm / 75mm perforated HDPE / Kaytech corrugated subsoil drainage pipes wrapped in non-woven geotextile fabric (bidim) with stone filter beds.
* **Erosion Control & Soil Retention:** Stone pitching in cement mortar, Reno mattresses, wire mesh gabion baskets, reinforced concrete retaining walls, and rumble strips.

### 6.3 Road Construction, Surfacing & Pavements
* **Pavement Layer Works:** Construction of imported dump rock sub-base, selected G7/G5 layers, stabilized C3/C4 cement-treated sub-base layers, and crushed stone G1/G2 base layers compacted to 98%–102% Mod AASHTO.
* **Asphalt Surfacing:** Continuous-graded medium/fine asphalt surfacing, asphalt overlays, mill-and-replace inlays, slurry seal application, and single/double chip-and-spray surfacing.
* **Road Maintenance & Rehabilitation:** Surface failure patching, pothole deep repairs, crack routing and hot elastomeric bituminous sealing, edge break rehabilitation, and road profiling.
* **Kerbing & Channeling:** Supply and machine/manual installation of precast concrete kerbing (Figure 8, Figure 8C mountable, Figure 7 semi-mountable, Figure 12 barrier kerb, Figure 3 barrier), concrete haunching, and in-situ cast channels.
* **Interlocking Block Paving:** Heavy-duty grey Type SA precast interlocking paving blocks (60mm for pedestrian sidewalks, 80mm for roadways, intersections, and bus rapid transit loading bays) laid on 20mm river sand bed with joint plaster sand broomed and compacted.
* **Road Ancillaries:** Road traffic signage, guardrails with terminal ends, thermoplastic retro-reflective road markings, road studs, and traffic calming speed humps.

### 6.4 Water Supply & Sewer Reticulation Networks
* **Potable Water Reticulation:** Excavation, flexible bedding, pipeline installation (uPVC, mPVC, HDPE, ductile iron, and welded carbon steel pipes), pressure testing, disinfection, and chlorination.
* **Individual Erf Connections:** Domestic and commercial metered water connections, saddle installations, water meter kiosks, and boundary valves.
* **Sewer Reticulation:** Gravity outfall sewer mains, vitrified clay / structured-wall uPVC sewer pipe laying, drop manholes, vacuum sewer lines, and municipal sewer network interconnections.
* **Mechanical Pump Stations & Reservoirs:** Construction of reinforced concrete booster pump stations, wet wells, high-lift pumps, mechanical pipework manifolds, control valve chambers, suction/delivery pipework, and groundwater storage reservoirs.

### 6.5 General Building & Housing Construction (NHBRC Grade 5)

* **Commercial & Institutional Infrastructure:** Office complexes, municipal depots, community centers, health clinics, and educational facilities (classrooms, ablution facilities, and administration blocks).
* **Full Trade Execution:** Formwork, reinforced concrete slabs, load-bearing bricklaying, internal/external plastering, structural timber/steel roof trusses, roof sheeting/tiling, ceiling boards, aluminium windows and doors, glazing, wall/floor tiling, painting, ironmongery, electrical reticulation, and complete plumbing installations.

### 6.6 Electrical Reticulation & Specialist Building Works

# 9. SHEQ POLICY FRAMEWORK (SAFETY, HEALTH, ENVIRONMENT & QUALITY)

### 9.1 Occupational Health & Safety (OHS Act 85 of 1993)
* **Zero Harm Target:** Zero fatalities and zero Lost Time Injuries (LTIs) across all active project sites.
* **Statutory Appointments:** Formal CR appointments for Section 16.2 managers, construction managers (CR 8.1), site safety officers (CR 8.5), risk assessors (CR 9.1), and health and safety representatives (CR 17.1).
* **Daily Toolbox Talks:** Mandatory daily safety briefings prior to shift commencement covering high-risk tasks (trenching, working at heights, heavy plant interaction, traffic control).
* **Mandatory PPE:** 100% compliance with SABS-approved hard hats, high-visibility reflective vests/overalls, steel-toe safety boots, eye protection, ear defenders, and specialized gloves.
* **Right to Refuse Work:** Any employee or subcontractor is empowered to immediately halt operations in the presence of unsafe trenching, compromised shoring, overhead hazards, or defective equipment without fear of reprisal.

### 9.2 Environmental Management (ISO 14001 Alignment)
* **Waste Separation:** Hazardous waste (used oil, chemicals, bitumen residue) contained in bunded areas and disposed of via certified hazardous waste handlers.
