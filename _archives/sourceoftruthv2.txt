SECTION 1 — COMPANY OVERVIEW
================================================================================
Makhaswa Holdings (Pty) Ltd is a fully/100% Black-owned civil engineering and
building construction company headquartered in Johannesburg/Midrand, Gauteng,
South Africa. Incorporated with the CIPC on 17 July 2017 as a private company

SECTION 10 — PROJECT DELIVERY METHODOLOGY (Source B only)
================================================================================
Executed under GCC 2015, JBCC, FIDIC, and NEC3 contract standards, in five
stages: (1) Initiation & Mobilisation, (2) Procurement & Resource Plan,
(3) Execution & Site Supervision, (4) Quality Assurance & SHEQ Control,
(5) Handover, Closeout & Defects Liability Period.

Quality control practices cited: SANAS-accredited third-party material
testing; Troxler nuclear gauge / sand replacement field density testing;
150mm concrete test cubes at 7/14/28-day compressive strength intervals;
continuous total station/GPS setting-out.
