Setup Instructions: Webmail (Titan)

Company Email
  1. Click Copy Signature above.
  2. Log in to your Webmail account.
  3. Next to your email address on the left, click the 3 dots (⋮) → Account settings.
  4. Scroll down in settings to Signatures and click Create custom signature.
  5. Enter a name (e.g. Makhaswa Official), click inside the signature box, and press Ctrl + V (Paste).
  6. Set it as default for new messages and replies, then click Save.
Formatting looks off after pasting? Click Copy HTML Code above, switch the Titan editor to HTML mode (</>), paste the code, click Preview, then Save.

Setup Instructions: Outlook Desktop

  1. Click Copy Signature above.
  2. In Outlook, go to File → Options → Mail → Signatures.
  3. Click New and type a name (e.g. Makhaswa Official).
  4. Click inside the signature editing box and press Ctrl + V (Paste).
  5. Select it under New messages and Replies/forwards, then click OK.

Setup Instructions: Outlook Web (365)

  1. Click Copy Signature above.
  2. Log in to outlook.office.com and click the Settings (Gear) icon.
  3. Go to Mail → Compose and reply → Email signature.
  4. Click + New signature, click inside the box, and press Ctrl + V (Paste).
  5. Set it as your default signature and click Save.

Setup Instructions: Gmail / Google

  1. Click Copy Signature above.
  2. In Gmail, click Settings (Gear) → See all settings.
  3. Scroll down to the Signature section and click Create new.
  4. Click inside the signature box and press Ctrl + V (Paste).
  5. Choose it under Signature defaults and click Save Changes at the bottom.

Setup Instructions: Apple Mail

  1. Click Copy Signature above.
  2. Open Apple Mail and go to Settings → Signatures.
  3. Click + to create a signature, and uncheck "Always match default message font".
  4. Click into the preview area on the right and press Cmd + V (Paste).
Quick Tip: Only want to add your signature to a single email? Click Copy Signature above, click at the bottom of your email message, and press Ctrl + V (or Cmd + V on Mac).
