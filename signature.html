Setup Instructions

Click "Copy Formatted Signature" above, then follow the instructions for your email application:

Outlook Desktop

  1. Click Copy Formatted Signature above.
  2. In Outlook, go to File → Options → Mail → Signatures.
  3. Click New and enter a title (e.g. Makhaswa Official).
  4. Click into the signature editing box and press Ctrl + V (Paste).
  5. Set under New messages and Replies/forwards, then click OK.

Outlook Web (365)

  1. Click Copy Formatted Signature above.
  2. Log into outlook.office.com and click the Settings (Gear) icon.
  3. Go to Mail → Compose and reply → Email signature.
  4. Click + New signature and press Ctrl + V (Paste).
  5. Set as your default signature and click Save.

Gmail / Google

  1. Click Copy Formatted Signature above.
  2. In Gmail, click Settings (Gear) → See all settings.
  3. Scroll down to the Signature section.
  4. Click Create new and press Ctrl + V (Paste).
  5. Set under Signature defaults and click Save Changes at the bottom.

Apple Mail

  1. Click Copy Formatted Signature above.
  2. In Apple Mail, open Settings → Signatures.
  3. Click +, uncheck "Always match default message font".
  4. Click into the signature preview area and press Cmd + V (Paste).
