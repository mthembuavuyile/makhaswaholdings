Who We Are

Makhaswa Holdings (Pty) Ltd is a fully black-owned civil engineering and construction company headquartered in Gauteng with active operational hubs in Pretoria, Midrand, Limpopo (Mokopane), and Durban (KwaZulu-Natal). Officially registered on 17 July 2017, the company has grown into a reputable multi-disciplinary contractor trusted by both public and private sector clients for the successful delivery of infrastructure and construction projects across South Africa.

With a strong focus on quality, efficiency, and reliability, Makhaswa Holdings consistently delivers projects on time and within budget while contributing to sustainable community development.

"We don't just construct buildings — we create lasting legacies. From concept to completion, we bring your vision to life with precision, passion, and excellence."
