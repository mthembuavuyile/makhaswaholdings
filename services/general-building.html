Building Construction Capability

Makhaswa Holdings (Pty) Ltd holds active CIDB Grade 6GB PE (General Building) and 7SB PE (Specialist Building) grading, alongside registration with the National Home Builders Registration Council (NHBRC Reg: 4000006217) as a Provisional Class 5 Home Builder.

We deliver turnkey structural projects under formal JBCC and GCC contracts, managing professional teams of architects, quantity surveyors, structural engineers, and statutory inspectors to ensure compliant, defect-free delivery.

All structural and building operations adhere strictly to the National Building Regulations, SANS 10400 standards, and occupational health and safety protocols.
