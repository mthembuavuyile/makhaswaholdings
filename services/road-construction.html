Civil Road Capability

Makhaswa Holdings (Pty) Ltd holds active CIDB Grade 8CE PE capability. Our road construction division delivers turnkey infrastructure solutions for state road authorities, provincial departments, and municipal panels.

We combine high-capacity road paving machinery with rigorous laboratory compaction testing. Our delivery model accommodates both greenfield route construction and high-traffic brownfield rehabilitation requiring comprehensive traffic accommodation plans (SARTSM Chapter 13).

All road layer works, asphalt surfacing, kerbing, and signalization are executed to COLTO and municipal engineering standards.
