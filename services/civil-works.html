Civil Infrastructure Delivery

Under our CIDB Grade 8CE PE accreditation, Makhaswa Holdings executes vital municipal and bulk water infrastructure across South Africa. We specialize in deep trenching, high-capacity pipeline installations, and structural hydraulic works.

Our operations comply strictly with SANS 1200 and municipal standard details. From precast box culverts to mass concrete retaining walls, each project is backed by comprehensive geotechnical assessments, trench shoring safety protocols, and certified pressure testing.

All pipeline and drainage installations are delivered in accordance with client engineering specifications and municipal requirements.

Core Scope & Deliverables

Our civil infrastructure division covers the full water, drainage, and structural earth retaining scope.

Stormwater Systems

Municipal drainage networks, culvert installations, and stormwater attenuation infrastructure.

  • Precast & cast-in-situ box culverts
  • Concrete stormwater pipes (class 50D/100D)
  • Catchpits, manholes & grid inlets
  • Attenuation ponds & outlet headwalls

Sewer & Water Pipelines

Bulk water distribution trunk mains and municipal sewer reticulation networks.

  • uPVC, HDPE, ductile iron & steel piping
  • Precast concrete sewer manholes
  • Air valve, scour & chamber installations
  • Hydrostatic pressure & air testing

Gabions & Mattresses

Flexible hydraulic structures and erosion protection along riverbanks, channels, and culverts.

  • Heavily galvanized wire mesh baskets
  • Geotextile filter fabric backing
  • Channel lining & energy dissipators
  • Embankment stabilization retaining works

Stone Pitching & Walls

Surface water velocity control and soil stabilization for cuttings, drains, and abutments.

  • Grouted and plain stone pitching
  • Concrete side drains & chute construction
  • Reinforced concrete retaining walls
  • Subsoil drainage & agricultural pipes
