Electrical Contracting Capability

Makhaswa Holdings holds active CIDB Grade 7EB PE accreditation, enabling our electrical division to execute municipal power distribution, substation civil installations, building electrical works, and arterial public lighting.

All electrical installations are executed and tested in strict compliance with SANS 10142-1 (Code of Practice for the Wiring of Premises) and the Electrical Installation Regulations under the Occupational Health and Safety Act, supported by official Certificate of Compliance (CoC) handovers.

Our team works closely with municipal authorities and consulting engineers to deliver safe, energy-efficient power reticulation and reliable illumination infrastructure.
